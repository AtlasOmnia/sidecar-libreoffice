Sidecar for LibreOffice 0.4.1 (LibreOffice extension)
by Atlas Omnia

Starts an authenticated loopback-only bridge inside LibreOffice. Hermes tools operate only on explicitly bound open Calc, Writer, or Impress documents.

The native Sidecar chat panel offers an explicit Light/Dark theme toggle. Dark is the fallback. The choice is saved only in this LibreOffice profile's user/hermes-bridge/sidebar-theme.json and applies to open Sidecar panels; no Hermes credentials are changed. If saving fails, a visible notice reports it without discarding conversation or draft content.
